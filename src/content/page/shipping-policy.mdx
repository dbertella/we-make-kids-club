I’m a one-person business (at least for now), so every order is packed with care by me. I aim to get your kit on its way as quickly as possible while keeping things sustainable and avoiding unnecessary packaging.

## Processing time

* Orders are usually prepared and dispatched within 1–3 working days (Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays).
* If I’m away, unwell, or experiencing an unusually busy period, I’ll update this page or contact you as soon as possible.
* You’ll receive an order confirmation by email. Once your parcel has been dispatched, I’ll send tracking details where available.

## Delivery times

Delivery estimates begin after your order has been dispatched, not from the moment you place it.

| Destination                     | Typical delivery time                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| UK standard delivery            | 2–5 working days after dispatch                               |
| UK express delivery, if offered | 1–2 working days after dispatch                               |
| International delivery          | Varies by destination; often 5–15 working days after dispatch |

These times are estimates provided by postal and courier services. I can’t guarantee delivery dates, especially during busy periods, bad weather, or customs delays.

## Shipping costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout and depend on the weight of your order and your delivery address. Any available free-shipping threshold will also be shown at checkout.

## International orders

For orders outside the UK, please note that import duties, taxes, and customs charges may apply. These are set by your country and are the customer’s responsibility. I’m unable to predict or pay these charges.

## Lost or delayed parcels

If your tracking shows your parcel as delivered but you haven’t received it, please check with household members, neighbours, and your local delivery office first. Then contact me at wemakekidsclub\@gmail.com within 14 days of the expected delivery date, and I’ll help you investigate with the postal service.

If a parcel is genuinely lost in transit, I’ll do my best to resolve it with the carrier and will either replace the kit or refund you once the carrier confirms it is lost.

## Incorrect or damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged or contains the wrong item, please email me at wemakekidsclub\@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery, with a photo of the item and packaging. I’ll sort out a replacement or refund as quickly as I can.

## Contact

Questions about your order? Please email wemakekidsclub\@gmail.com and include your order number. As this is a small business, I may not reply instantly, but I’ll get back to you as soon as I can.
