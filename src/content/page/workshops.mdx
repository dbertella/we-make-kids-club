**Creative eco-fashion workshops for every setting**

I design and deliver hands-on eco-fashion workshops that use sewing, repair, upcycling and creative making to explore sustainability in a practical, engaging way.

Whether you are a school, youth organisation, community group or workplace, I can create a session that fits your people, space and goals.

**Where I’ve worked**

I have delivered creative eco-fashion workshops for:

Primary schools, youth organisations and community places, Netflix offices.

Each session is tailored to the group, but all are built around creativity, practical skills and a more thoughtful relationship with clothing.

### SCHOOLS

**Creative eco-fashion workshops for primary schools**

I offer curriculum-linked and enrichment workshops that introduce children to sustainable fashion through hands-on making.

Sessions can support learning around:

* Textiles and design technology
* Sustainability and fast fashion
* Fine motor skills and practical making
* Teamwork and problem-solving

Workshops can be delivered as:

* One-off workshops
* Half-day or full-day sessions
* Multi-week programmes
* Special events, such as Earth Day or Arts Week

### COMMUNITY GROUPS

**Creative sessions for youth and community organisations**

I work with youth organisations, community groups and local projects to deliver inclusive, practical workshops that build confidence and creative skills.

Sessions can explore:

* Upcycling and repair
* Personal style and identity
* Fast fashion and sustainability
* Sewing and textile skills
* Collaborative making projects

These sessions can be designed for children, young people, families or mixed-age community groups.

### WORKPLACES

**Creative wellbeing and sustainability workshops**

I offer relaxed, hands-on workshops for workplaces that want to bring creativity, sustainability and team connection into the room.

Sessions can include:

* Visible mending and clothes repair
* Upcycling and creative reuse
* Sustainable fashion conversations
* Collaborative textile projects
* Seasonal making sessions

These workshops work well for team days, family days, wellbeing programmes, volunteer events and sustainability initiatives.
