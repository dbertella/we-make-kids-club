## SCHOOLS

### Creative eco-fashion workshops for primary schools

I offer curriculum-linked and enrichment workshops that introduce children to sustainable fashion through hands-on making.

Sessions can support learning around:

- Textiles and design technology
- Sustainability and fast fashion
- Fine motor skills and practical making
- Teamwork and problem-solving

Workshops can be delivered as:

- One-off workshops
- Half-day or full-day sessions
- Multi-week programmes
- Special events, such as Earth Day or Arts Week
