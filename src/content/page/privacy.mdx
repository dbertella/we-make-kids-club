**Privacy Policy for We Make Kids Club**

Last updated: November 11, 2026. 

This privacy policy describes how We Make Kids Club collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website wemakekidsclub.com or any of our services.

**Information We Collect**

We collect the following types of personal information:

* Contact information (e.g., name, email address, phone number)
* Demographic information (e.g., age, location)
* Usage data (e.g., pages visited, time spent on site)
* Device information (e.g., IP address, browser type)

**How We Collect Information**

We collect information in the following ways:

* Directly from you when you provide it to us
* Automatically as you navigate through our website
* From third-party sources, such as our business partners

**How We Use Your Information**

We use your personal information to:

* Provide and improve our services
* Communicate with you about our products and services
* Personalize your experience on our website
* Analyze website usage and trends
* Comply with legal obligations

**Information Sharing and Disclosure**

We may share your personal information with:

* Service providers who assist us in operating our website
* Law enforcement or other governmental agencies when required by law
* Business partners with your consent

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

**Data Security**

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

**Your Rights**

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

* Access your personal information
* Correct inaccurate information
* Delete your personal information
* Object to or restrict certain processing activities
* Data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

**Children's Privacy**

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

**Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

#### About the Store

What information I collect

When you place an order or contact me, I may collect:

* Your name
* Email address
* Delivery address
* Order details
* Payment confirmation information
* Messages you send me

I do not see or store your full card details. Payments are processed securely through Stripe.

How I use your information

I use your information to:

* Process and fulfil your order
* Send order confirmations and updates
* Post your kit to the correct address
* Respond to your questions
* Keep records of orders and payments
* Meet legal and accounting requirements

Who I share information with

I only share the information needed to run my business and complete your order.

This may include:

* Stripe, to process payments securely
* My website platform, to store order information
* Royal Mail or another delivery service, to post your order
* My email provider, to send order confirmations and replies

I do not sell your personal information.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at [wemakekidsclub@gmail.com](mailto\:wemakekidsclub@gmail.com).
