# Five simple upcycling projects kids can make at home

I've gathered here five of my favourite upcycling projects I tested with children from 5 to 10 years old. The youngest one might prefer the no-sewing projects, although I met some five-year-olds very keen to sew.

These projects are great because you get to reuse what you probably already have and they can be adapted to all ages from very simple to more detailed finishing touches. Even adults can have fun making any of those.

1. T-shirt into Necklace (no-sewing)
2. T-shirt into reusable bag (no-sewing)
3. Loom Knit Old tights and socks (no-sewing)
4. Fabric scraps into Christmas baubles (or pocket hugs)
5. Long sleeves into pouches
6. Toddler trousers into Bag

So let me show you how easy each of these eco-fashion project is!

### 1. From T-Shirt to Necklace

What you need: an old t-shirt 

To be honest, you only need the bottom of a t-shirt so you could even crop a T-shirt and you'd have enough to make a necklace with what you cut.

![](</upcycled necklace from tshirt.png>)

It was the theme of my first ever eco-fashion workshop! Boys and girls from 5 to 12 year old had fun cutting their old T-shirt and create their own stylish necklace.

How to make a necklace out of a t-shirt?
\- Grab your old Tee and a good pair of scissors
\- Cut the bottom hem (because it's not elastic)
\- Then cut horizontal strips (loops) of about 2-3cm and stretch them out!
\- Keep the loops or cut them to make some plaits or a mix. Go wild.
\- The closure? Just cut a rectangle of fabric, tie it to your necklace, roll it around hiding your knot, then slide the end of your fabric under it. Et voilà! No sewing needed but you might want to add a drop of hot glue gun to secure the knots.
