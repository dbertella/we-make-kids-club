A beginner-friendly sewing kit for children and new makers. This handmade, eco-friendly kit includes simple materials to get started with sewing and makes the perfect gift for every creative child.

***

> *I wanted a starter sewing kit that feels precious , not plasticky, and that is also unique. Something to cherish and make you want to actually use it.*

This pocket-sized kit is for little makers with big ideas. Each pouch is handmade from beautiful reclaimed fabric, giving existing textiles a new purpose: a little home for their sewing essentials.

Your kit contains:

* 2 sewing needles
* 3 pins
* 1 needle threader
* 1 safety pin
* Thread/yarn (x3)
* handmade pouch (8.5x9x2cm)

Fold it up, snap it shut and keep it ready for your next stitching adventure.

*Note: Fabrics and threads are reclaimed and may vary.*

**For ages 8+. Adult supervision required. Contains sharp needles, pins and small parts.**
