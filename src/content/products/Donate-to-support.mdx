Wondering how to support eco-fashion?

You can make a donation to help me source more material, but saving fabrics is even better!

You can always donate fabrics and sewing material. Old clothes (especially 8+ years) are also used all the time for children to upcycle and experiment.

If you work with fashion, we highly appreciate fabric swatches and samples too!

I try to source second-hand material and reclaimed fabric as much as I can, as I think there's already so much available that I don't want to encourage more production and create more waste.

Send an email to [wemakekidsclub@gmail.com](mailto\:wemakekidsclub@gmail.com) if there's anything you'd like to donate.
