Some notes about your kit:

* **Fabric scissors** are not included. I'm a small business and I'm trying to keep the product affordable. I highly recommend good fabric scissors as they make cutting fabric much easier but you might already have some strong all-purpose scissors that will do the job. If you need advice on what fabric scissors to buy for your child, I'll be happy to share some recommendations.
* **Markers** are not included. I use water-erasable or heat-erasable markers all the time during my workshops, but to get started, you can use any pen (preferably on the back of the fabric).
* **Stuffing** is not included. Why? Because I don't want you to buy more polyester fibrefill (derived from petroleum) when you can reuse old fabric to make your own stuffing. Children can use the fabric remnants from their kit and any old clothes/socks cut into small pieces. An extra activity that is also sustainable!
* **Fabrics and threads are reclaimed and may vary**. If you want to know exactly what the pouch you're buying will look like, send me a DM on Instagram @wemakekidsclub.
