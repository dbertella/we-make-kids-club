A sewing kit to make your own eco-friendly softie and more. Each pouch contains reclaimed fabrics and yarn to make a cute little softie.

Inside your kit:

* 2 pieces of fabric (for the body)
* 1 piece of fabric (for belly and eyes)
* Yarn (\~5m)
* Needle
* Totoro-looking sewing pattern to cut (one medium, one small)
* 2 extra sewing patterns to cut
* Instruction booklet with step-by-step guide and illustrations
* Handmade pouch with snap button (26x19)

“This kit is not just about making a softie. This kit teaches you the power of reusing.” 

Follow the instructions and use what is inside to make your softie. Then repeat using the extra sewing patterns, or making your own patterns and reusing old fabrics.

Not included: scissors, marker and stuffing.

Did you know? You can make your own stuffing using small pieces of fabric scraps! Don’t hesitate to use the leftover fabrics from your kit and check around your house for old fabric to cut (t-shirt, socks, cloths, anything!) Who needs more ‘plastic’ fillings?
